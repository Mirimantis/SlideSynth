# 10.2 Rolling Buffer + "Keep That" Retrospective Capture — Build Plan

Dedicated build plan for BACKLOG 10.2 (Phase 10, [performance-jam-looper-plan.md](../../performance-jam-looper-plan.md)). Follows 10.1 (free-running jam clock), which is its foundation: because the clock runs, buffered gestures carry **musical-time** positions and drop straight onto the grid with no quantize step.

## Goal

While jamming, a rolling buffer captures everything you play. A **"keep that"** key commits the phrase you *just* played into a curve — after the fact. Serendipity isn't armed in advance.

## Decisions (settled 2026-07-25)

- **Keep semantics: newest uncommitted phrase.** A *phrase* is one sounding span (LMB press → release, or MIDI noteOn → noteOff). Each press of Keep commits the most recent phrase not already committed; pressing twice walks backward and keeps the two most recent. No modes, no window math — and armed recording needs no special-casing, since its phrases are already committed and Keep simply finds nothing.
- ~~**Target is the newest *closed* phrase.**~~ **Revised during testing (2026-07-25): keep takes the newest uncommitted phrase, open or closed.** The closed-only rule was shipped first and failed the first real test: a line held across the loop point leaves the post-wrap half *open*, so Keep skipped it and committed the sealed pre-wrap half instead — the part the player had just performed was unreachable until release. Keeping an in-progress phrase now **splits** it: the samples so far commit, and capture continues into a fresh phrase seeded with the last sample, so nothing is dropped and the note is never broken. Phrases too short to fit a curve are stepped over rather than silently consumed.
- **No quantize.** The phrase lands exactly where it was played. The running clock already placed it in musical time; rubato is preserved.
- **Keep never touches the loop region.** No "first take defines the loop" — the region stays explicit (dragged markers today, live taps in 10.6). No new untouched-region state.
- **Buffer survives session stop, with a visible indicator.** Stopping the clock does not discard keepable material, so "wait, that last bit was good" still works. A **Keep button** in the transport lights when keepable phrases exist and is disabled when not — it is both the indicator and the mouse affordance.
- **Key: `K`** (free; `J` = jam, `R` = record).
- **Scope: every sounding voice in any perform context** — jam, plain scroll-canvas playback, and armed record all fill the buffer. One code path, no mode checks.
- **Window: 30 s, wall-clock.** A constant (`KEEP_BUFFER_SECONDS`) for now; a Transport slider can come later if it proves worth tuning.

**The load-bearing problem:** `finalizeCurve` taking *the whole buffer* is only correct because capture starts at LMB-down. A continuously-running buffer breaks that. Phrase spans fix it and unify both paths.

## Implementation

### 1. Phrases replace flat buffers (performance-engine.ts)

```ts
interface RecordedPhrase {
  samples: RecordedSample[];
  open: boolean;        // still capturing
  committed: boolean;   // already turned into a curve
  closedAtMs: number;   // wall clock, for eviction
}
```
Per-voice storage becomes `Map<VoiceId, RecordedPhrase[]>`. New/changed engine API:

- `beginPhrase(voiceId)` — open a phrase (LMB down, MIDI noteOn, loop-wrap reopen).
- `captureSample(voiceId, sample)` — append to the open phrase; opens one defensively if none.
- `closePhrase(voiceId)` — mark closed, stamp `closedAtMs` (LMB up, noteOff, loop wrap, session stop).
- `takeNewestUncommittedPhrase(voiceId, opts)` — mark committed and return its samples. `opts.requireClosed` distinguishes the two callers: the armed-release path takes the phrase it just closed; Keep requires `closed`. Replaces `finalizeCurve`'s buffer-draining role (curve building stays in `curveFromRecording`).
- `getKeepableVoices()` / `getKeepablePhraseCount()` — drives the Keep button's enabled state and the indicator.
- `getRecordingBuffers()` — keep the existing read-only shape for the live-trail renderer (8.18); it should surface the **open** phrase's samples so trails render unchanged.

**Eviction is wall-clock, not beat-based** — loop wraps run the beat counter backwards, so beat comparisons misbehave at exactly the moment a looper is busiest. Drop phrases whose `closedAtMs` is older than `KEEP_BUFFER_SECONDS`; never evict an open phrase. Hard backstops: max phrases per voice and the existing total-sample cap.

**`stopSession()` no longer clears everything** (that's the "survives stop" decision): it closes open phrases and keeps committed/uncommitted history for eviction to age out. `startSession()` clears — a new session starts fresh.

### 2. Capture continuously (main.ts)

`captureComposeRecordingSample` gate drops the `recordArmed` requirement: capture whenever `phase === 'playing'` and a voice is actually **sounding** (LMB held in perform, or a held MIDI note). Silent cursor movement is never captured — "what was just played" means what was heard, and it keeps the buffer small.

Phrase lifecycle wiring:
- `beginPhrase` on the perform `mousedown` (beside `composeEngine.onLmbDown`) and on MIDI noteOn.
- `closePhrase` on `mouseup` / noteOff / `composePerformStop`.
- **Loop wrap:** in the `onLoopWrap` handler, close and immediately reopen phrases for still-held voices — the un-armed mirror of the 8.21 armed behavior. This guarantees no phrase ever spans the seam, so no phrase carries a backwards beat jump, and a kept phrase that straddled the boundary lands as two contiguous curves exactly like armed recording.

### 3. The keep action

`keepLastPhrase()` in main.ts, mirroring `finalizeComposeRecordedCurves` and sharing its commit/grouping logic (extract the common tail rather than duplicating it):

1. Collect the newest closed uncommitted phrase across non-MIDI voices; if a multi-voice Prism cluster was sounding, take the sibling phrases from the same span so they commit as one `groupId` group (reusing the existing `createGroupId()` + `voiceIndex` stamping).
2. `history.snapshot()` **once per keep** — this is exactly the 10.4 decision ("each kept pass = one undo entry"). Note the engine's `sessionHistorySnapshotted` debounce is designed for the armed-session model and must be bypassed here, or repeat keeps would collapse into one undo step.
3. Build curves via `curveFromRecording` and push onto the selected track (MIDI phrases → MIDI-armed track), matching today's routing. 10.3 changes this to a new track per pass.
4. Toast confirming what was kept (e.g. duration in beats); toast on failure too ("nothing to keep", or phrase too short — `curveFromRecording` returns null under 0.05 beats).

## Tests & verification

**Unit — extend [src/canvas/performance-engine.test.ts](../../src/canvas/performance-engine.test.ts)** (created in 10.1):
- Phrase lifecycle: begin → capture → close produces one closed phrase; capture with no open phrase opens one.
- Keep takes the newest closed uncommitted phrase; a second keep walks backward; a third with nothing left returns null.
- An open phrase is never taken when `requireClosed`.
- Armed-release regression: `takeNewestUncommittedPhrase` after `closePhrase` returns exactly that gesture's samples and no earlier history.
- Eviction: a phrase closed beyond `KEEP_BUFFER_SECONDS` is dropped; an open phrase never is.
- `stopSession` preserves keepable phrases; `startSession` clears them.

**Full suite:** `npx tsc --noEmit` + `npx vitest run` green (455+ tests; no format/schema change expected — phrases are runtime-only).

**Manual (dev server, port 5187):**
1. Jam un-armed, play three separate phrases, press `K` twice → the two most recent commit as curves at the beats they were played; the first remains keepable.
2. Keep with nothing played → button disabled, `K` shows the "nothing to keep" toast.
3. Prism draw-mode chord cluster → one keep commits all voices as a group (select one, whole cluster selects).
4. Phrase crossing a loop seam with Loop on → keeps as two contiguous curves at the boundary.
5. Play a phrase, Stop the jam, then press `K` → still commits (survives-stop), and the Keep button stayed lit after Stop.
6. Armed recording regression: arm, record a gesture, release → exactly one curve, no over-capture of pre-press history; `K` afterwards finds nothing to keep.
7. Undo: two keeps → two `Ctrl+Z` presses remove them one at a time.
8. Leave a jam idle > 30 s after a phrase → keepable indicator goes dark (evicted).

## Out of scope

Layer-per-pass tracks (10.3 — keeps land on the selected track for now), the undo-last-layer key (10.4; this item only guarantees one-snapshot-per-keep), deliberate record-next-full-pass (10.5), live loop taps (10.6), continuous dynamics on kept phrases (11.1 — volume stays the flat 0.8 lane), raw-take retention of the buffer (12.4).
